What this proves — and what it doesn't

This offline prototype demonstrates a possible workflow: categorisation, priority flags and draft replies. It uses simple keyword rules, not a trained AI model. It has no access to Origin Coffee systems or customer data and cannot send messages. A real pilot would require an agreed business need, privacy review, authorised integration and testing against staff decisions.
